Help with Wrenford Rescue

Getting started

Choose Begin Mission, read the incident and objectives, then select Start Rescue. Crew biographies and the map legend are optional. The hands-on guide can be replayed from How to Play.

Planning a rescue

Move with the board or direction controls. Stabilise injured people, begin an escort, then reach a marked exit. Each responder has their own action points and abilities. Read the forecast before ending a round; undo is available only within the current round.

Optional rescues and medals

The required people determine mission completion. Optional rescues appear separately. Complete the bonus before evacuating the last required person: the mission ends immediately when all required people are safe.

Flood water

Responders can wade alone. Only water rescue technicians such as Bryn can escort through water or begin an escort there. Water cannot be suppressed and puts out fire it covers.

Progress and recovery

Your current mission is saved locally. Return to Headquarters to continue it later. If a save is damaged, the app tries its previous local copy and explains any recovery. Reset Mission Progress removes the active rescue and completed record, including local recovery copies; use it only when you intend to start again.

Audio and accessibility

Music, Sound Effects and Haptics can be changed independently in Settings. Gameplay works without sound. The game supports larger text, VoiceOver and reduced motion; response controls provide an alternative to grid interaction.

Reporting a problem

Record the mission name, round, device model, iOS version and app version, along with what happened and what you expected. Do not include personal information in screenshots. To report a problem or ask for help, email wrenford@paulbillings.co.uk.
