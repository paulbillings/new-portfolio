Wrenford Rescue

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Your rescue record stays on your device

Wrenford Rescue works offline. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. The app does not collect or send your gameplay or personal information to the developer.

What is stored

The app stores mission progress, the current rescue, tutorial history and your audio and haptic preferences locally. Previous save copies help recover damaged progress. This data remains until you reset it or remove the app; device backups are managed by your Apple device settings.

Your choices

Use Settings → Reset Mission Progress to delete the active rescue and completed rescue record, including local recovery copies. Audio and haptic preferences are preserved. You can change those preferences independently in Settings.

External links

Audio Credits contains links to the music source and licence. Opening an external link takes you to a third-party website, which has its own privacy practices. Gameplay does not require opening these links.

Contact

Wrenford Rescue is developed by Paul Billings. If you have any questions about this policy, email wrenford@paulbillings.co.uk.
