Perfect Parcel

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Perfect Parcel is a puzzle game developed by Paul Billings, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. There are no player accounts or sign-in. Your game progress is saved on your device. Advertising and purchase providers process information as described below.

Your game progress

The game stores coins, hints, completed parcels, workshop purchases, settings, daily-gift history and story progress locally using Unity PlayerPrefs. The game does not upload this progress to a developer-operated server, and there is no account-based cloud save. Deleting the app removes its local save; Apple may retain device backups under your backup settings. Removing the app does not cancel or refund App Store purchases.

Advertising and your choices

Perfect Parcel uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between parcels. Buying Remove ads or the Christmas bundle stops the between-parcel ads; optional rewarded ads remain available.

Google's advertising software may process your IP address and approximate location, device and app identifiers, device information, ad views and interactions, and crash and performance information to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud and maintain its services. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is available only when you allow tracking through Apple's permission prompt. Declining tracking does not prevent all advertising-related data processing.

Where required, Google's consent form asks for your advertising choices. Use Settings → Privacy & ad choices to reopen Google's form where available, or open the app's iPhone settings. You can also manage tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Consent choices and local requirements determine which advertising features are available.

Read Google's partner-sites policy and Google's privacy policy for its purposes, retention and privacy controls.

Purchases and Unity IAP

Apple processes payments through the App Store. I do not receive your payment-card details. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to receive purchase confirmations, provide purchased items and restore eligible purchases.

Unity IAP 5.4 and later may process purchase and transaction information, device or installation identifiers, technical information and approximate country to operate the purchase service and report revenue. Unity retains transaction information as needed to operate that service, including preventing duplicate processing. See Unity's game-player privacy policy and IAP privacy information.

Apple handles store and payment records under Apple's privacy policy. Restore purchases does not restore your locally saved puzzle progress or spent consumable coins and hints.

Analytics and diagnostics

The game has no developer-operated gameplay analytics. Unity Analytics, Cloud Diagnostics and engine diagnostics are disabled in the current project. This does not disable the advertising and purchase providers' own processing described above. Apple may share crash reports and usage information according to your device privacy settings.

Support correspondence

If you email perfect-parcel@paulbillings.co.uk, I receive your email address, message and any attachments. I use these to answer your question, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. My email provider processes this correspondence to deliver and store it. I keep correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and any applicable record-keeping obligations. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary personal information.

External websites

Opening Privacy policy or Help & contact takes you to my website in your browser. Website hosting providers, including Netlify, may process connection information such as your IP address and requested URL to serve and secure the pages. External links to Google, Unity and Apple are governed by their own privacy practices. These information pages do not add analytics scripts or contact forms.

Children

Perfect Parcel is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for the App Store Kids category. AdMob is configured to request general-audience ads with a maximum content rating of G. If you believe a child's personal information has been collected inappropriately, contact me so I can investigate with the relevant provider.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal information, object to processing, request portability or withdraw consent. Contact perfect-parcel@paulbillings.co.uk for requests concerning support correspondence or data processed on my behalf. I cannot recover or remotely change your device-only save. Google and Apple provide controls for information they hold; I can help route a request to the appropriate provider, including Unity.

Where UK or EEA law applies, support correspondence is processed for the legitimate interest of responding to requests and maintaining the game; necessary purchase processing supports delivery of purchases. Advertising consent is requested where required. Providers explain their own legal bases in their policies. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. You may complain to your local regulator, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes and contact

I will update this policy and its date when the game's privacy practices change. For help or questions about this policy, email perfect-parcel@paulbillings.co.uk.
