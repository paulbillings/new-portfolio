Short description

Perfect Parcel is a cosy Christmas packing puzzle for iPhone. You're the new elf in the Lapland post room: pack every awkward pile of gifts into its box with not a single gap, then spend the coins you earn fixing Santa's Workshop, which the new sleigh crashed straight through on its test flight.

Long description

Christmas runs on goodwill, and goodwill is running low. The brand-new sleigh sputtered on its test flight and crashed through the roof of Santa's Workshop. Every parcel packed with care sends a little goodwill back out into the world, and the coins it earns pay to put things right before Christmas Eve.

Each parcel is a small, hand-made puzzle. Gifts drop into a side-on box under gravity, you drag them in and tap to turn them, and the box has to be filled exactly: gifts and bubble wrap, no gaps. Each puzzle has one answer. New rules arrive as you go: fragile gifts need padding beside them, heavy gifts can't sit on fragile ones, and "this way up" gifts can't be turned. Undo is always free, and there are no timers and no fail screens.

When a parcel is packed you fold the lid, pull the tape and thump down the ON TIME FOR XMAS stamp. Then it's back to the workshop to patch the roof, mend the chimney and fix the window, before decorating it with a tree, a train set and a star on top, while the sky outside fills with stars and northern lights.

Your guide is Postmaster Robin, an over-excited robin in a postman's cap who thinks every day is the best day ever. The whole game is made to look like a felt diorama, like a little stop-motion set.

  • 116 hand-checked parcels, each with exactly one solution
  • Repair, then decorate Santa's Workshop with 18 items
  • Plays offline, no account needed
