Help with Perfect Parcel

Packing a parcel

Drag gifts from the tray into the box. Tap a gift to rotate it where rotation is allowed. Pieces settle under gravity. Fit every gift and piece of padding into the fixed box, filling every square, then seal the parcel. Undo is free and unlimited.

Special gifts and hints

Fragile gifts need padding touching them. Heavy gifts cannot sit directly on fragile ones. Gifts marked this way up cannot rotate. New rules are introduced as you progress. A hint shows one correct placement; completing a parcel without hints earns the full packing bonus.

Repairing the workshop

Packing parcels earns coins for repairs and decorations. Open the workshop, choose an available item and check its coin cost before buying. Finish a room to unlock the next chapter. Music, sound effects, vibration and movement can be changed in Settings.

Progress and daily gifts

Progress is saved on this device. There is no sign-in or account-based cloud save, and reinstalling the app can remove your progress, coins and hints. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step unless you accept losing that save. Device backups depend on your Apple settings. Return to the home screen to collect an available daily gift. Daily gift reminders are optional: turn them on in Settings and allow iPhone notifications. You can receive a note at 6pm local time if the gift is still waiting, with at most three upcoming reminders after your last visit. Collecting the gift skips that day’s reminder; switching reminders off cancels pending notes. Tapping a note opens the home screen and available gift. If permission was refused, enabling reminders opens iPhone notification settings. Focus, Scheduled Summary and other iPhone settings can delay or silence alerts.

Ads and purchases

Rewarded ads are optional and need an internet connection and an available ad. Remove ads and the Christmas bundle stop ads between parcels; optional reward ads remain available. Purchase prices and approval are supplied by the App Store. If the store or an ad is unavailable, check your connection and try again later. A purchase waiting for parental approval may arrive after approval.

Restoring purchases

Use Restore purchases in Settings or the Shop while signed into the Apple Account used to buy. This can restore eligible non-consumable purchases such as Remove ads. It does not restore spent consumable hints or coins, or your puzzle and workshop progress. If something you paid for is missing, contact support with the product name, approximate purchase date and what happened. Never send full card details or your Apple Account password. Refund requests are handled by Apple.

Reporting a problem

Email perfect-parcel@paulbillings.co.uk. Include your app version (shown at the bottom of Settings), iPhone model, iOS version, parcel number or workshop room, what you did, what happened and what you expected. A screenshot can help; remove personal information before sending it. Perfect Parcel is developed by Paul Billings.

Privacy and ad choices

Read the privacy policy for information about local saves, advertising, purchases and support emails. Settings → Privacy & ad choices opens the available consent controls or the app's iPhone settings. Tracking permission can also be changed in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
