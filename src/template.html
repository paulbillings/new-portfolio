Perfect Parcel

Cosy Christmas packing puzzle

First release · Free on iPhone this December

The world has grown grumpy and the Christmas spirit is running low. Without it, the new sleigh crashed straight through Santa's Workshop. Pack every parcel perfectly, earn coins and fix it all before Christmas Eve. Postmaster Robin needs you!

  • Drag, drop and rotate gifts until the box is perfectly full
  • Fragile, heavy and this-way-up gifts change the puzzle as you go
  • Spend your coins repairing, then decorating, the workshop
  • Over a hundred parcels to pack
